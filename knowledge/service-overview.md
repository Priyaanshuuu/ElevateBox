# E-commerce Website Development Service

We build custom e-commerce websites for small and medium businesses.

## Typical scope

- Product catalog pages
- Product detail pages
- Cart and checkout
- Payment gateway integration (Razorpay, Stripe)
- Order and customer management
- Admin panel for product and order updates
- Mobile responsive UI
- Basic SEO setup

## Optional integrations

- WhatsApp chat and notifications
- Inventory sync
- Coupon and discount rules
- Delivery partner integrations
- Analytics and conversion tracking

## Delivery approach

- Requirement discovery
- UX and wireframe planning
- Development and QA
- Go-live support
- Post-launch maintenance options

## Typical timelines

- Basic store: 2 to 4 weeks
- Medium complexity store: 4 to 8 weeks

## Typical budget framing

Budget depends on complexity, integrations, and timeline urgency.
A discovery call is required before final quotation.
